## Website brief

### Project name

**Enterprise AI Adoption Framework** website.[^1]

### Project purpose

Build a professional, visually strong, simple website that tells the story of how a Design Science Research Methodology study investigated the mismatch between probabilistic AI and deterministic organisations, built two prototype artefacts—GridSense and OsciProbity—and from that work derived reusable enterprise frameworks and artefacts.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^1]

The website must:

- establish the enterprise AI problem clearly,[^1]
- present the research credibly through DSRM,[^2]
- demonstrate that real artefacts were built,[^1]
- and position the resulting frameworks and future report/artefacts as valuable, reusable outputs.[^3][^4][^5][^6]


### Primary audience

The main audience is:

- enterprise architects,
- CIOs and CTOs,
- AI governance leads,
- innovation leaders,
- transformation leaders,
- regulated-sector decision makers,
- and serious practitioners interested in enterprise AI operating models.[^1]

A secondary audience is:

- academic readers,
- design science researchers,
- and potential collaborators or buyers of the future report and artefact pack.[^2][^1]


### Core message

The core message of the site is:

> Organisations struggle with AI not because they are resistant to innovation, but because AI is probabilistic while enterprises are built for determinism, governance, and accountability.[^1]
> This research used DSRM to investigate that tension through two artefacts—GridSense and OsciProbity—and produced a set of reusable frameworks showing how organisations can explore AI transformatively, govern it incrementally, and bridge the path between the two.[^4][^5][^6][^3][^2][^1]

***

## Product decisions

### Version 1 decisions

- Public website, no login.[^7][^8]
- Four primary pages.[^1]
- Demo videos of GridSense and OsciProbity embedded on Page 3.[^1]
- Page 4 designed to support future monetisation, but not an aggressive sales page yet.[^1]
- Soft CTA such as contact, register interest, or “report in preparation” on Page 4.[^1]


### Future-ready decisions

The site should be architected so it can later support:

- a paid research report,
- a downloadable artefact pack,
- a payment link,
- and possibly gated premium content.[^9][^1]

But **V1 should not require user accounts or authentication**.[^10][^7]

***

## Narrative strategy

The site must follow a deliberate four-step narrative:

1. **Problem** — AI as an unpredictable beast; enterprises as deterministic systems.[^1]
2. **Research** — DSRM used to investigate the problem through building.[^2][^1]
3. **Proof** — GridSense and OsciProbity as tangible artefacts, with demos and build methodology.[^1]
4. **Outputs** — frameworks and artefacts as reusable outcomes, with room for future commercialisation.[^5][^6][^3][^4]

The frameworks must feel **arrived at through research**, not simply drawn as abstract concepts.[^2][^1]

***

## Page architecture

### Page 1 — The Beast and the Gap

**Purpose:** Create tension, urgency, and conceptual clarity.[^1]

**Key themes:**

- AI as powerful but unpredictable.[^1]
- Organisations as governed, rule-bound, accountable systems.[^1]
- The mismatch between probabilistic AI and deterministic enterprise structures.[^1]
- Two ways forward emerged: transformative and improvement.[^1]
- A transition path is needed between them.[^5][^1]

**Desired effect:**
The visitor should immediately understand the problem and want to see how it was researched.

### Page 2 — Research Through DSRM

**Purpose:** Give the work legitimacy and methodological credibility.[^2]

**Key themes:**

- Why DSRM was used.[^2]
- The research question.[^1]
- GridSense and OsciProbity as research artefacts.[^1]
- Transformative vs structured/governed development modes.[^1]
- How the four frameworks emerged from the artefact journey.[^6][^3][^4][^5]

**Desired effect:**
The visitor should believe the frameworks were earned through research, not opinion.

### Page 3 — Built Artefacts

**Purpose:** Show concrete proof that the research produced real systems.[^1]

**Key themes:**

- GridSense demo video and explanation.[^1]
- OsciProbity demo video and explanation.[^1]
- AI-assisted development methods.[^1]
- GridSense as exploratory, vibe-coding, transformative build mode.[^1]
- OsciProbity as structured, requirement-led, architecture-first, spec-driven build mode.[^1]
- Technology footprint and tools used.[^1]

**Desired effect:**
The visitor should feel this is real engineering work, not conceptual storytelling.

### Page 4 — Frameworks and Artefacts

**Purpose:** Present the reusable outputs and support future monetisation.[^3][^4][^6][^5]

**Key themes:**

- Enterprise AI Framework.[^5]
- Hourglass Model.[^4]
- Enterprise AI Guardrails Framework.[^3]
- AI Engineering Disciplines Framework.[^6]
- Additional artefacts over time.[^1]
- Future report / artefact pack / register interest area.[^1]

**Desired effect:**
The visitor should see long-term value, reuse potential, and a reason to engage further.

***

## Tone and style

### Tone

The tone should be:

- professional,
- intelligent,
- modern,
- credible,
- and visually confident.[^1]

It should avoid:

- hype,
- startup cliché,
- generic AI futurism,
- and overclaiming production success.[^1]


### Tone progression by page

- **Page 1:** dramatic, metaphorical, thought-provoking.[^1]
- **Page 2:** structured, credible, research-led.[^2]
- **Page 3:** practical, technical, evidence-based.[^1]
- **Page 4:** clear, reusable, advisory-oriented.[^4][^6][^3][^5]

***

## Design direction

### Overall aesthetic

The website should feel premium, editorial, and enterprise-grade rather than “AI startup template.” It should be visually impactful but restrained.[^1]

### Desired attributes

- Appealing and polished.
- Impactful hero sections.
- Simple information architecture.
- Strong typography.
- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Dark/light mode support if feasible.
- High-end professional finish.[^1]


### Avoid

- neon gradients,
- purple AI clichés,
- generic SaaS feature grids,
- overdecorated backgrounds,
- and overuse of animations.[^1]


### Visual concept

- Page 1 can be darker, more cinematic, more metaphor-driven.[^1]
- Pages 2–4 should progressively become cleaner, calmer, and more structured.[^2][^1]

***

## Content principles

### Message discipline

Every page should answer one question:

- Why this problem matters.
- Why this research is credible.
- What was actually built.
- What others can reuse.[^1]


### Framework credibility rule

Every framework should be introduced as something that **emerged from the work**:

- “This framework emerged from…”
- “This became necessary when…”
- “This was distilled from…”[^1]


### Evidence rule

Do not imply live enterprise rollout success where there was only prototype demonstration and positive initial stakeholder feedback. Be strong on:[^1]

- feasibility,
- speed,
- quality of thinking,
- artefact build,
- and reusable design logic.[^1]

***

## Functional requirements

### Must-have

- Four primary pages or sections.[^1]
- Clear top navigation.[^1]
- Embedded demo videos on Page 3.[^1]
- Framework images and explanations on Page 4.[^6][^3][^4][^5]
- Mobile responsive layout.
- Fast loading.
- Public access, no login.[^8][^7]
- Contact / interest CTA on Page 4.[^1]


### Nice-to-have

- Smooth scrolling or section transitions.
- Subtle animations.
- Sticky navigation.
- Download or enquiry CTA placeholder for future report.[^1]


### Not in V1

- Login/authentication.[^7][^10]
- Full ecommerce flow.
- Member dashboard.
- Gated downloads.

## Build methodology

### Development approach

The website will be built using **100% AI-generated coding and AI-assisted testing**, with no manual coding or manual code review. The quality bar remains high: the site must still feel intentional, coherent, professional, and robust.[^1]

### Important framing

This website is itself part of the broader story:

- AI can support rapid communication artefacts as well as product prototypes.[^1]
- GridSense represents open, transformative AI-enabled building.[^1]
- OsciProbity represents structured, specification-led AI-enabled building.[^1]

***

## Success criteria

The website is successful if it does the following:

- Clearly explains the enterprise AI problem in a memorable way.[^1]
- Makes the DSRM research process understandable and credible.[^2]
- Shows that GridSense and OsciProbity are real and meaningful artefacts.[^1]
- Gives the four frameworks legitimacy by showing how they emerged from the work.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Feels polished enough for enterprise stakeholders and potential buyers.[^1]
- Creates interest in future detailed reports, artefact packs, or advisory engagement.[^1]
